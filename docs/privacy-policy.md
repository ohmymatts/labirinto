# Privacy Policy — LABIRINTO

Effective date: 2026-02-11

LABIRINTO ("the game") is a browser-based maze game.

**Data we collect: none.**

- The game performs no analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No account system exists; nothing is sent to or stored on any server.
- Your best level is saved **only on your own device** using the browser's
  local storage. Deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser)
  erases it permanently.
- No third-party services, SDKs, or cookies are used.
